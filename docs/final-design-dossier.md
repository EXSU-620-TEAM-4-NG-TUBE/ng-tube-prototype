# Final design dossier

## IX. Traceability and limitations (Deliverable D9)

The bench prototype demonstrated measurement, selected alarm and stop behaviours, and audit retrieval. The revised service adds reason-specific phone messages for completed technical results and extends notification eligibility to normal and advisory results. Four-outcome phone verification remains open.

The prototype agreed with the comparison meter within ±0.30 pH at three tested points. Videos support selected LED and stop functions; the connection-loss image shows the browser withholding pH. The weekly interval and physical fault-detection limits are summarized in the requirement dispositions.
